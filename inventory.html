Inventory Management

Upload a CSV with one row per container (up to 500 rows). Required columns: name, container_size, unit (g, mg, kg, mL, L) and location, written as the full path, e.g. Main Building / Room 101 / Cabinet A, (> also works as a separator), or a unique location name. Dates may be YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY. Each row gets its own generated barcode.
